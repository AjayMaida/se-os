# User Personas

This document defines the primary user personas for **SE-OS**, their objectives, pain points, daily workflows, and platform expectations.

---

## 🎓 Persona 1: The College Student / New Graduate

### Profile
- **Name:** Maya Sharma
- **Age:** 21
- **Current Status:** Final-year Computer Science undergraduate
- **Target Role:** Entry-Level Software Engineer (FAANG / Tier 1 Tech)
- **Time Commitment:** 20–25 hours per week

### Objectives
- Clear campus placement coding rounds and technical interviews.
- Build a strong portfolio of non-trivial, production-quality projects beyond generic university homework.
- Master Core Data Structures, Algorithms, and Operating Systems fundamentals.

### Pain Points
- Overwhelmed by fragmented resources (LeetCode, YouTube tutorials, random roadmaps).
- Decision paralysis: doesn't know which problems to solve or when they are "interview ready".
- Difficulty balancing college coursework with competitive programming.

### Ideal SE-OS Journey
1. Takes the onboarding assessment; inputs target companies (Amazon, Microsoft, Google).
2. Receives a structured 16-week DSA and CS Fundamentals roadmap.
3. Wakes up to 3 daily focused tasks (e.g., 2 medium Two-Pointer problems, 1 OS thread safety article).
4. Tracks streak and GitHub contributions directly in the SE-OS Dashboard.

## 💼 Persona 2: The Career Switcher / Mid-Level Transitioner

### Profile
- **Name:** Alex Miller
- **Age:** 28
- **Current Status:** Full-Stack Developer at a legacy IT services company (3 years experience)
- **Target Role:** Senior Backend Engineer at a product-led tech company
- **Time Commitment:** 10–12 hours per week (evenings and weekends)

### Objectives
- Transition from routine CRUD maintenance to high-throughput distributed systems.
- Master System Design, event-driven architectures, caching patterns, and database tuning.
- Optimize resume and GitHub portfolio to pass resume screening at top startups.

### Pain Points
- Extremely limited time; cannot afford 4 hours a day on random tutorials.
- Needs high-signal, practical engineering drills rather than academic theory.
- Unsure what gap separates a Mid-level developer from a Senior level hire.

### Ideal SE-OS Journey
1. Inputs current stack (Node.js, PostgreSQL) and target stack (Go/Python, Kafka, Distributed Systems).
2. AI roadmap generates targeted System Design milestones (Raft consensus, Sharding, Message Queues).
3. Daily planner gives concise 45-minute daily micro-drills that fit around a busy workday.
4. Uses AI Mentor to review architecture trade-offs for real-world scenarios.

## 🚀 Persona 3: The Senior Specialist / Staff Aspirant

### Profile
- **Name:** Marcus Vance
- **Age:** 35
- **Current Status:** Senior Software Engineer (8+ years experience)
- **Target Role:** Staff Software Engineer / Principal Architect
- **Time Commitment:** 5–8 hours per week

### Objectives
- Deepen domain expertise in platform scalability, multi-region resiliency, and AI integration.
- Prepare for Staff+ behavioral and architectural leadership interviews.
- Keep abreast of emerging paradigms (LLM agents, vector databases, WASM).

### Pain Points
- Existing interview prep sites only cater to junior algorithm grinding.
- Lack of nuanced, high-level architectural feedback and trade-off exploration.

### Ideal SE-OS Journey
1. Configures Staff Engineer track targeting complex distributed systems and AI systems.
2. Interacts with the AI Mentor for mock architectural evaluations and trade-off critiques.
3. Uses Interview Studio to simulate architectural presentations and cross-functional leadership discussions.
